# Instructions

- Be exact with amounts, currencies, and dates.
- Keep financial accounts, people, companies, subscriptions, and trips as distinct entities. Represent balance-sheet values as current superseding observations and weekly net-worth snapshots, not mutable asset entities.

- Keep summaries concise and lead with what changed.
